About Us

Dedicated to Your Recovery Journey

Al Fajr Physiotherapy & Rehabilitation Centre has been a trusted cornerstone of healthcare in Ras Al Khaimah. Located in Al Safa Building, Al Nakheel, we provide evidence-based physiotherapy and rehabilitation services to patients of all ages.

Our licensed specialists combine the latest clinical techniques with personalised care, helping you regain function, reduce pain and restore your quality of life.

Conditions We Treat

Pain Relief & Recovery

From shoulder dislocations to back pain and knee injuries, our specialists deliver targeted treatment and full rehabilitation.

Shoulder
Shoulder Pain & Dislocation

Rotator cuff tears, shoulder dislocations, frozen shoulder and impingement syndrome. We restore full joint stability and range of motion.

Book Treatment →
Back
Back Pain & Spine

Herniated discs, sciatica, lower back pain, postural problems and spinal stenosis. Manual therapy and targeted exercises for lasting relief.

Book Treatment →
Knee
Knee Pain & Dislocation

Knee dislocations, ACL/PCL tears, patella problems, arthritis and post-replacement rehab. We stabilise the joint and rebuild strength.

Book Treatment →
Neck
Neck Pain & Cervical

Cervical spondylosis, whiplash, stiff neck, tension headaches and nerve impingement. Manual therapy and mobilisation restore normal movement.

Book Treatment →
Hip
Hip Pain & Joint

Hip bursitis, arthritis, labral tears and post hip-replacement rehab. We address pain, improve mobility and restore comfortable walking.

Book Treatment →
Sports
Sports Injuries

Sprains, ligament tears, muscle injuries and stress fractures. Our sports rehab programme gets athletes back to full performance quickly and safely.
